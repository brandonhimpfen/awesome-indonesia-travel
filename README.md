## Official & Planning

Essential official government portals, tourist boards, entry visa information, and weather services.

- [Wonderful Indonesia (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.indonesia.travel/) - Official national travel guide featuring destination highlights, cultural itineraries, and travel advisories.
- [Ministry of Tourism, Indonesia](https://kemenpar.go.id/en) - Official federal ministry portal providing tourism regulations and national development updates.
- [Indonesia e-Visa Portal (Directorate General of Immigration)](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/) - Official government electronic visa platform for tourist visa applications (VoA and pre-arranged visas).
- [BMKG Weather (Agency for Meteorology, Climatology, and Geophysics)](https://www.bmkg.go.id/) - Official national weather service providing forecasts, seismic updates, and tropical cyclone tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating domestic airlines, inter-island ferries, trains, and regional transport.

- [Garuda Indonesia](https://www.garuda-indonesia.com/) - National flag carrier operating domestic connections across Indonesian archipelago hubs.
- [Lion Air Group](https://www.lionair.co.id/) - Major domestic low-cost airline network covering primary and secondary airports.
- [KAI (Kereta Api Indonesia)](https://www.kai.id/) - National railway operator offering intercity passenger train services across Java and Sumatra.
- [PELNI (National Passenger Ship Company)](https://www.pelni.co.id/) - State-owned maritime transport network operating large passenger vessels between major island ports.

## Destinations & Regions

### Bali & Nusa Tenggara

- [Bali Tourism Board (Dispar Bali)](https://disdar.baliprov.go.id/) - Official regional portal for cultural guidelines, visitor tax details, and temple etiquette.
- [Mandalika Tourism Hub (Lombok)](https://kemenpar.go.id/en) - Official guide for exploring southern Lombok, coastal beaches, and the international circuit region.

### Java & Jakarta

- [Wonderful Jakarta](https://www.indonesia.travel/) - Municipal visitor guide for exploring the capital's historic Old Town (Kota Tua), museums, and rapid transit networks.
- [Borobudur & Prambanan Heritage Guide (In Journey Destination)](https://injourney.id/) - Official management portal for visiting world-famous UNESCO Buddhist and Hindu temple complexes in Central Java.
- [Mount Bromo National Park Portal](https://kemenpar.go.id/en) - Official visitor guide for Tengger Caldera sunrise treks and active volcano tours in East Java.

### Komodo & Flores

- [Komodo National Park Official Guide](https://www.indonesia.travel/) - Visitor portal for Labuan Bajo, pink beaches, diving routes, and Komodo dragon conservation rules.

### Sumatra & Lake Toba

- [Lake Toba Tourism Authority](https://kemenpar.go.id/en) - Official regional guide for exploring the world's largest volcanic lake, Samosir Island, and Batak heritage.

### Sulawesi & Raja Ampat

- [Wakatobi Marine National Park Guide](https://kemenpar.go.id/en) - Official portal for world-class coral reef diving and marine conservation in Southeast Sulawesi.
- [Raja Ampat Tourism Info](https://kemenpar.go.id/en) - Visitor portal for exploring West Papua's biodiverse marine paradises, karst islands, and homestays.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Indonesia's remote work visa pathways.

- [Indonesia Second Home & Remote Worker Visas (E-Visa Immigration)](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/) - Official government immigration guidelines for long-term remote work stays.
- [Canggu & Ubud Coworking Networks](https://www.indonesia.travel/) - Regional guides highlighting digital nomad hubs, community coliving spaces, and networking events in Bali.

## Safety & Nature Regulations

Practical advice on emergency contacts, local customs, volcanic safety, and marine conservation.

- [Indonesia Emergency Assistance (Police & Ambulance)](https://www.indonesia.travel/) - National emergency contact lines (Dial **110** for Police and **112** for general integrated emergency response).
- [Center for Volcanology and Geological Hazard Mitigation (PVMBG)](https://vulkanologi.esdm.go.id/) - Official agency tracking active volcano alert levels and restricted exclusion zones.
